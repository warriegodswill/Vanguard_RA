# Vanguard Revenue Assurance (RA) Platform

Automated multi-schema reconciliation engine for businesses using POS, bank, and inventory data.

Vanguard Revenue Assurance helps business owners identify revenue discrepancies, transaction mismatches, inventory variances, and potential capital leakage by reconciling data from multiple business systems.

## Overview

Small and medium-sized businesses often manage sales, banking, and inventory records separately.

This makes it difficult to quickly identify:

- Missing or unmatched transactions
- Differences between POS sales and bank deposits
- Inventory discrepancies
- Duplicate transactions
- Revenue reporting inconsistencies

Vanguard addresses this by standardizing data from different sources and running automated reconciliation checks.
